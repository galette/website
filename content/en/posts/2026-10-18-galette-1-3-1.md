A fresh batch just out of the oven: here is Galette 1.3.1! Along with the expected fixes, this release brings a few improvements you asked for, mainly on groups and saved searches.

## Easier groups management

Member's groups are no longer managed from modal windows: the member form shows a table of their groups, where you can switch "member" and "manager" on or off, remove a group or add a new one:

{{< img_link path="/site/assets/images/screenshots/1.3.1/member-groups-en.png" alt="Groups table on the member form" >}}

The same goes for the group form: members and managers are listed in a single table. Changes are highlighted until you save them:

{{< img_link path="/site/assets/images/screenshots/1.3.1/group-members-en.png" alt="Members and managers table on the group form, with a member just added" >}}

Members can also be attached to groups from CSV imports, and their groups are exported as well.

## Saved searches that stay up to date

Advanced search dates can now be **relative to the search day**: "today", "start of the month", "end of the membership period"... plus or minus a number of days, weeks, months or years. Save a search on members whose membership ends within 30 days, and it will still be right next month:

{{< img_link path="/site/assets/images/screenshots/1.3.1/saved-search-dates-en.png" alt="Advanced search: fixed modification dates, and due date between today and today plus 30 days" >}}

Saved searches can also be renamed, or updated with the current criteria. They load properly whatever the language, they keep their groups criteria, and a warning is displayed when a criterion refers to something that no longer exists.

## Clearer passwords

The password strength indicator lists the rules, and which ones are met. It is now displayed on the member form as well:

{{< img_link path="/site/assets/images/screenshots/1.3.1/password-strength-en.png" alt="Password strength indicator with its rules" >}}

The web installer gives advice for the super administrator password, and refuses it under 6 characters.

## And also…

* Member picture is configured like other fields: its visibility can be set, and it can be made mandatory
* Members CSV export includes dynamic fields
* Required fields are better enforced: title, dropdowns, dynamic fields files; a read-only required field is no longer reported as missing
* Preview features can be turned on from advanced configuration, or with the `galette:feature:enable` and `galette:feature:disable` commands, without switching to debug mode
* Copy to clipboard buttons for the two-factor secret, the recovery codes and the system information
* The member actions menu is the same on display and edit pages
* The self subscription form always shows email, login and captcha
* With membership extension, a contribution added on the last day of membership now starts the day after
* Group PDF export displays all subgroups, not only the first level
* Required text areas are displayed properly
* HTML tags are removed from titles
* Messages are no longer lost when a background request runs before the page they were meant for
* A loader is displayed while the dark mode stylesheet is generated
* New `galette:twofactor:reset` command, and several fixes on console commands
* ...

## Before upgrading

There is no database change in this release, and 1.3 plugins remain compatible. As always, **backup your database** before upgrading!

See [Galette 1.3.1 changelog](https://doc.galette.eu/en/develop/changelog.html#v1310) for all details.
