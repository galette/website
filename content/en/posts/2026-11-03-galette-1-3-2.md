A fresh batch just out of the oven: here is Galette 1.3.2! Along with the expected fixes, this release makes mailings, contributions and the members list easier to work with… and brings news about two plugins.

## Easier mailings

Mailing recipients are no longer managed from a table of all members: search a member by name or ID to add them, remove one with a click:

{{< img_link path="/site/assets/images/screenshots/1.3.2/mailing-recipients-en.png" alt="Mailing recipients management: searching a member to add, and the recipients list" >}}

Your mails and PDF models can also contain **links to your documents**: a specific document, all documents of a same type, or the public documents page. And what you insert in them is finally displayed exactly as you typed it.

## Mass change on contributions

As for members, select contributions in the list to change them all at once: payment type, date, comment and dynamic fields. Only checked fields are changed, and they get checked as soon as you change their value:

{{< img_link path="/site/assets/images/screenshots/1.3.2/contributions-mass-change-en.png" alt="Mass change of 3 contributions: payment type and comment checked" >}}

Members mass change no longer empties the yes/no dynamic fields you did not select.

## A members list to your taste

Members dynamic fields can now be displayed in the members list: add them from the core lists configuration. Added, moved or removed fields are highlighted until you save, and a field removed by mistake can be kept back with a click:

{{< img_link path="/site/assets/images/screenshots/1.3.2/core-lists-en.png" alt="Members list configuration: a dynamic field added, a field about to be removed" >}}

From the list and from advanced search, you can also find members that do not belong to any group, and advanced search can look for empty, or filled, fields.

## Up to date plugins

Galette lets you know when a new release of one of your plugins is available: on the plugins page, in the menu, and when an administrator logs in:

{{< img_link path="/site/assets/images/screenshots/1.3.2/plugins-updates-en.png" alt="Plugins list: a new release is available for two of them" >}}

## Fullcard and PayPal are no longer maintained {#fullcard-paypal-1-3-2}

Two plugins are leaving the menu:

* **Fullcard** replaced the built-in membership card model; it turned out to be of little use.
* **PayPal** relies on an integration PayPal shuts down in January 2027. No feedback came after this shutdown was announced: we consider the plugin is no longer used. For online payments, turn to the [Stripe](https://galette-plugins.github.io/plugin-stripe/) or [HelloAsso](https://galette-plugins.github.io/plugin-helloasso/) plugins.

Their repositories have been archived, and a note says so in their README and on their website.

## And also… {#and-also-1-3-2}

* Company name is printed on labels, between name and address
* Logging out does forget the groups managed by the member
* Galette starts again when its directory has been renamed
* Dashboard no longer fails when the browser does not send its display cookie
* Installer tells which directories cannot be written, and why
* ...

## Before upgrading {#before-upgrading-1-3-2}

There is no database change in this release, and 1.3 plugins remain compatible. As always, **backup your database** before upgrading!

See [Galette 1.3.2 changelog](https://doc.galette.eu/en/develop/changelog.html#v1320) for all details.
