A small batch, but don't let it get cold: here is Galette 1.3.3! Nothing new on the menu this time, but fixes, some of them security related. **Upgrading is recommended.**

## On the menu {#on-the-menu-1-3-3}

* Security related fixes
* Storing child members
* Searches on PostgreSQL
* Dynamic fields creation
* Number of records per page
* Self subscription
* Mass change
* ...

## Plugins side {#plugins-1-3-3}

[Activities](https://galette-plugins.github.io/plugin-activities/) and [Events](https://galette-plugins.github.io/plugin-events/) plugins have also been updated recently to fix minor issues - but your Galette (1.3.2) should already have told you ;)

## Before upgrading {#before-upgrading-1-3-3}

There is no database change in this release, and 1.3 plugins remain compatible. As always, **backup your database** before upgrading!

See [Galette 1.3.3 changelog](https://doc.galette.eu/en/develop/changelog.html#v1330) for all details.
