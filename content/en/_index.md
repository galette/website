## How to start?

* **On your hosting**: PHP and a MariaDB or PostgreSQL database are enough, a shared hosting will do ({{< doclink path="installation/prerequis.html" >}}prerequisites{{< /doclink >}}).
* **With Docker or YunoHost**, if you already run your own server ({{< doclink path="installation/other_methods.html" >}}other installation methods{{< /doclink >}}).
* **At a non-profit hosting provider** from the [CHATONS](https://www.chatons.org/search/by-service?field_software_target_id=311) collective, ready to use.
