Announcing the general meeting, sending this year's cards, sharing the minutes of the last board meeting… An association spends a lot of time communicating with its members. Galette relies on your members file so that each message, each document reaches the right people.

## Targeted mailings

Everything starts from the members list: filter (up to date members, a group, those living in a town, those whose membership is about to expire…), select, and choose **Mail**.

* Write as text or HTML with the built-in editor, add **attachments**, **preview** before sending.
* Choose the **sender**: the one from the association preferences, the logged-in person, or another address.
* **Save** a mailing to reuse it as a template, with its recipients.
* Large mailings are **split into batches** and spread over time to respect your mail server quotas, with a page following their progress.

Members without an email address are not forgotten: Galette proposes to print their labels.

## Emails that go by themselves

Some messages do not need you: registration confirmation, new contribution notice, lost password, and the [membership fees reminders]({{< relref "fees.md" >}}) before and after the due date. Their texts are provided, and you can rewrite them your way. The staff can also be notified of every new registration or contribution.

## Cards, labels and attendance sheets

From the same members selection, Galette produces PDF documents ready to be printed:

* **membership cards**, with your texts, your logo, the year or the due date, and colors depending on the member status; every up to date member can also download their own from their space,
* address **labels**, set to the format of your sheets,
* **attendance sheets** for a general meeting or an event, with or without pictures,
* **adhesion forms**, blank or pre-filled, for those who prefer paper.

Receipts, invoices and adhesion forms follow **PDF models** you adapt: header, footer, layout.

## Share the association documents

Statutes, internal rules, meeting minutes: upload them into Galette and choose who can see them: everyone, logged-in members, group managers, staff or administrators only. Members find them in the public pages, with no email exchange and no link lying around.

## And also

Galette **charts** give an overview of the association: members by status, state of dues, contributions over time. Enough to prepare the annual report of the general meeting.
