## Chat live {#instant-messaging}

The rooms are open to everyone and every topic. Answers are not always instant: people drop by when they can, the main developer included.

* <span id="matrix"></span>**Matrix**: [#galette:matrix.org](https://matrix.to/#/%23galette:matrix.org), with any Matrix account. Only messages posted after you joined are visible.
* <span id="discord"></span>**Discord**: [the Galette server](https://discord.gg/98g2e3xka8).
* <span id="irc"></span>**IRC**: `#galette` on `irc.oftc.net`. The channel is bridged with Matrix; without a client that stays connected, you won't see messages sent while you were away.

## Mailing lists {#mailing-lists}

* [galette-users](https://lists.mailman3.com/postorius/lists/galette-users.mailman3.com/): help and general discussions,
* [galette-devel](https://lists.mailman3.com/postorius/lists/galette-devel.mailman3.com/): technical and development topics.

You must be subscribed to post on a list: from the list page, subscribe with your email address or a Mailman account. Archives are online, and you can reply from there too: [users](https://lists.mailman3.com/hyperkitty/list/galette-users@mailman3.com/), [development](https://lists.mailman3.com/hyperkitty/list/galette-devel@mailman3.com/). Discussions older than March 2024 are on [The Mail Archive](https://www.mail-archive.com/search?l=all&q=galette&a=1).

## Bugs and feature requests {#bugs-and-feature-requests}

Before opening a ticket on the [bug tracker]({{% param "galette_tracker_url" %}}), check that nobody did already: look at the [open bugs](https://bugs.galette.eu/projects/galette/issues?query_id=2) and the [open tasks](https://bugs.galette.eu/projects/galette/issues?query_id=3). The FAQ explains {{< doclink path="faq/index.html#how-to-report-a-bug-or-ask-for-a-feature" >}}how to write a good bug report or feature request{{< /doclink >}}.

## Social networks {#social-networks}

Follow the project on Mastodon: [@galette@framapiaf.org](https://framapiaf.org/@galette). This is not the right place to ask for help: prefer the channels above, where answers benefit everyone.
